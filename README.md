# Yen-Nan (Aaron) Ho

**AI Engineer** in Taiwan · Data Scientist at Harbor Tech

I turn industrial signal analysis into products people can operate: equipment monitoring, diagnostic reporting, and end-of-line quality inspection. My work spans product and domain design, system integration, customer delivery, and the development environment that helps people and AI agents work together.

## Industrial AI: From Field Data to Product Delivery

### Harbor AI 2.0 Industrial Intelligent Monitoring Platform

I lead product and domain design, connecting vibration and partial-discharge (PRPD) analysis, equipment status, diagnostics, scheduling, reports, and operator workflows. With the team and AI agents, I built much of 2.0's functionality from scratch while handling coexistence and gradual migration from 1.0. As of October 2026, 2.0 operates independently of 1.0.

### Harbor AI 2.0 Equipment End-of-Line Quality Inspection Platform

I led the customer project from requirements and specifications through system implementation, UI/UX refinement, and on-site demonstration and training, working with AI agents and engineering partners. When the initial UI met specifications but required deep, circular navigation, I reorganized it around plan-list, work-order, and board views, and added sensor-installation checks before measurement.

### Human–AI development environment

I built traceable project and issue records linking problems, decisions, implementation, and verification. AI agents develop, repair, and verify in parallel on isolated branches; daily functional regression feeds the maintenance loop. I set quality criteria, review domain assumptions, and work with reviewers on merge and release decisions.

## Selected Public Projects

### [MCIENet — DNA-sequence-based chromatin interaction prediction](https://github.com/changlabtw/MCIENet)

My master's thesis and most complete research project: research design, multi-scale CNN architecture, data-pipeline integration, ablation studies, cross-dataset evaluation, and explainability analysis. Some preprocessing was adapted from ChINN.

### [Horus — Multi-camera human identification](https://github.com/aaron1aaron2/Horus)

A team project combining tracking, person re-identification, and face recognition. I primarily owned ReID integration, sampling and matching, similarity thresholds, and tracking-record orchestration, using FastReID as the base model. The team won first place in Soochow University's capstone competition. [Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Fw6MSzFeSw)

### [Stock recommendation chatbot — E.SUN Fugle](https://github.com/aaron1aaron2/E.SUN_Fugle_project)

I proposed the topic, led team coordination, and designed, implemented, and deployed the Telegram chatbot that integrated teammates' recommendation modules. Our team received an Excellence Award in the 2020 Financial Programming Course Competition.

### [Disaster-resource dashboard — 2025 Taipei City Dashboard Hackathon](https://github.com/aaron1aaron2/2025-Taipei-City-Dashboard-Teamwork)

I contributed topic ideation and data collection, using a friend's dashboard-template fork with the team. The team advanced to the final.
